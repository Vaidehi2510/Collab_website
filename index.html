We’re actively polishing two areas as Collab continues to improve, with progress tracked here.

Review release highlights ↑
  1. 01 Issue #291

    Local models may fail without an NVIDIA GPU.

    On machines without an NVIDIA GPU, launching a local model can sometimes fail. Use a Hub-hosted model as a workaround.

    Local models · NVIDIA GPU recommended
  2. 02 Issue #293

    PDF content is not yet available to models.

    Collab does not currently parse PDF contents for model use. PDF parsing and context support are in progress.

    Files · PDF parsing
